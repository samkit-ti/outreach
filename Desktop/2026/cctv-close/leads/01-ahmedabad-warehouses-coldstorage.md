## Caveats

- Web-search quota ran out mid-task; the Mehsana/Kadi/Chhatral potato belt and Kheda/Bareja agri godowns are under-covered (only GCSA/blog listings, unverified). Textile/apparel godowns (Narol) returned only rental listings, no owner-run operator with a public name.
- GCSA's directory loads member rows via a POST form that returned no data to a plain fetch; the names above for GCSA members come from Google snippets and a 2013 mirror of the list. Re-check the live site before quoting capacity.
- Director names are from MCA-mirror sites (Zauba/Tofler/InsiderBiz/IndiaMART director tab) as of their last crawl; confirm the current board on mca.gov.in before naming anyone in a message.
- No personal phone numbers were collected; all numbers are the company's own published business lines.
