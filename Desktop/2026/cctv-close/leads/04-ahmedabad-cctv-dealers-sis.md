## How to pitch a dealer

1. **Lead with margin on the AMC they already bill.** Position the add-on as a per-site annual subscription they invoice alongside the AMC; show the arithmetic at 25% dealer margin on a 50-camera factory and how it survives the hardware price war Hikvision/CP Plus dealers lose every year.
2. **"No hardware change" is the whole first sentence.** Dealers lose deals when a customer must rip out working Hikvision/Dahua/CP Plus NVRs; make clear it reads existing RTSP/ONVIF streams or NVR exports and nothing on-site is replaced or re-cabled.
3. **Differentiate from the free Hik-Connect / CP Plus app alarms they already give away.** Those push motion/line-cross alerts; your pitch is "type a question, get the clip" (forensic search) plus a daily summary the factory owner reads on WhatsApp — things the OEM app does not do and the dealer cannot charge for today.
4. **Give them an AMC-renewal upsell script.** Dealers touch each industrial customer at renewal; hand them a 2-line pitch ("same cameras, now searchable; 2-week trial on your Kathwada plant") and a 2-site pilot LOI template so the ask is bounded and free.
5. **Protect their account.** Indian dealers fear being disintermediated; write territory/account protection and "dealer bills the customer, you bill the dealer" into the channel sheet before the first meeting.
