## 3. Buying mechanics

1. **Channel**: Large city/ICCC/Safe City contracts go via state tenders (often e-procurement) to an MSI; smaller police buys (Rs 5–20 cr) increasingly on **GeM** (e.g., Delhi Police 338-camera FRT bid, GeM, 30 Jul 2026). [IFF](https://internetfreedom.in/a-face-recognition-camera-ring-was-ordered-around-ramlila-maidan-ten-weeks-later-it-became-the-protest-site/)
2. **STQC / MeitY Essential Requirements (ER)**: Mandatory for CCTV from 9 Apr 2025; from 1 Apr 2026 no sale of non-ER cameras; government departments formally barred from buying non-ER kit; certification refused for Chinese-origin chipsets/firmware. Effect: Chinese share collapse, CP Plus share doubled in one year. [Matrix explainer](https://www.matrixcomsec.com/stqc-certification-er-compliance-for-cctv-cameras/); [Business Standard, 30 Mar 2026](https://www.business-standard.com/industry/news/india-ban-chinese-cctv-government-security-concerns-hikvision-dahua-market-126033000316_1.html)
3. **Make in India (PPP-MII Order 2017, amended 2020)**: Only Class-I/II local suppliers eligible; sample TCIL bid requires local content: cameras 60%, switches 60%, servers 60%, workstations 50%, VMS "locally developed". Purchase preference to Class-I for bids under Rs 200 cr. [TCIL bid doc](https://www.tcil.net.in/tender/pdf/25f2715_1.pdf)
4. **L1 dynamics**: Two-stage (technical qualify, then lowest price). Bengaluru Safe City tender re-floated 4 times before Honeywell won. CAG Karnataka (Report 11 of 2025) found tender conditions "tweaked" to select an ineligible Local SI in Shivamogga, nominal Rs 2,000/day penalty instead of contractual rate, and a KMDS data centre unfinished after 5 years (Rs 19 cr spent of Rs 60 cr). [CAG Report 11/2025](https://cag.gov.in/uploads/download_audit_report/2025/Report-11-of-2025_English-PA-on-SCM-06a9ffc486381d1.26439690.pdf)
5. **Eligibility walls**: Railways coach CCTV requires prior 800-camera + ICCC experience; NHAI unified ATMS software requires 1 x Rs 30 cr / 2 x Rs 20 cr / 3 x Rs 15 cr past projects; 9-year O&M. These exclude startups as primes. [IHMCL RFP 2026](https://ihmcl.co.in/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Volume-1_Unified-NHAI-ATMS-SW-For-Publish_260526.pdf)
6. **Contract shape**: Capex (2 yrs) + Opex/AMC (5–9 yrs) typical (Bengaluru 2+5; NHAI 1+9; Chandigarh Rs 1 cr/month ops). Video analytics specified as feature line items / per-channel licences in BOQ: ANPR, RLVD, FRT, crowd density, loitering, camera tamper, abandoned object, intrusion, people counting (Guwahati RFP addendum; Sagar ICCC+ITMS RFP; Ahmedabad SCADL 2026-27 O&M RFP). [Guwahati](https://gscl.assam.gov.in/sites/default/files/swf_utility_folder/departments/smartcity_webcomindia_org_oid_7/latest/addendum12changesinclausesofrfp.pdf); [Ahmedabad SCADL RFP](https://www.tcil.net.in/tender/pdf/26f2958_1.pdf); [Sagar RFP](https://www.tenderfiles.com/tenderdocuments/82026/4/bd946559-b770-420a-bdca-f979e1a3d50c?FileName=ICCCITMSRFP.pdf)
7. **Payment risk**: Chandigarh MC did not pay BEL for 13 months (~Rs 16 cr) after SPV closure; BEL switched off feeds and e-challan. Nagpur MC must fund ICCC O&M from own budget. Lucknow Safe City cameras reported non-functional but not disclosed. [Tribune, Jul 2025](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/chandigarh/functioning-of-command-control-centre-hit-as-bel-stops-data-service/); [Pulitzer Lucknow](https://pulitzercenter.org/stories/watched-unprotected-how-lucknows-safe-city-project-fails-women)
8. **Spec quality**: Delhi Police FRT tender had no SOP, no PIA, no accuracy threshold, no retention rule; police confirmed no ISO 27001 was asked. Inference: analytics is bought as a checkbox, not an outcome; vendors are not paid on accuracy. [IFF](https://internetfreedom.in/a-face-recognition-camera-ring-was-ordered-around-ramlila-maidan-ten-weeks-later-it-became-the-protest-site/); [The Wire](https://thewire.in/government/delhi-police-ai-facial-recognition)

**Failures / audit findings**
- Delhi: 2.64 lakh PWD cameras installed, 32,000+ (≈12%) non-functional, 15,000 never installed; audit ordered Sept 2025; PWD seeking Rs 100 cr for 50,000 more. [Millennium Post, 23 Sep 2025](https://www.millenniumpost.in/delhi/over-32000-cctvs-non-functional-628392); [Communications Today](https://www.communicationstoday.co.in/over-7500-cctv-cameras-found-non-functional-in-delhi-safety-audit/)

## 5. Regulation and risk

- **DPDP Act 2023 + DPDP Rules 2025**: Section 17(1)(c) exempts processing for prevention/detection/investigation of offences; 17(2)(a) lets Centre notify state instrumentalities for near-total exemption; Rules 2025 add "necessary and proportionate" + security safeguard conditions. As of Feb 2026 no instrumentality formally notified under 17(2)(a). CCTV facial data is biometric under the rules, so private operators (malls, RWAs, SI as data processor) carry obligations even where police do not. [Tsaaro](https://tsaaro.com/blogs/regulatory-carve-outs-examining-exemptions-under-the-dpdp-act-2023-and-dpdp-rules-2025); [Medianama, Nov 2025](https://www.medianama.com/2025/11/223-dpdp-act-citizens-cctv-surveillance/); [ELP, Jul 2026](https://elplaw.in/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Government-Exemptions-and-the-Balance-between-Privacy-and-State-Interests.pdf)
- **No FRT-specific statute**; IFF Project Panoptic tracked 120+ government FRT tenders by 2024. [IFF Panoptic](https://internetfreedom.in/update-on-iffs-projectpanoptic/)
- **Courts**: S.Q. Masood v State of Telangana (W.P. PIL 10/2022) — India's first FRT challenge, notice issued, still pending per Mar 2026 academic review. SC suo motu on police CCTV (2025). [IFF](https://internetfreedom.in/telangana-high-court-issues-notice-in-indias-first-legal-challenge-to-the-deployment-of-facial-recognition-technology/); [IJIRL, Mar 2026](https://ijirl.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/JUDICIAL-RESPONSE-TO-FACIAL-RECOGNITION-UNDER-RIGHT-TO-PRIVACY-IN-INDIA.pdf)
- **Political/reputational**: Delhi Police FRT ring around Ramlila Maidan tendered 10 weeks before it became the designated protest site; Vajra riot vans with 4K cameras + GPU under "no written procedure" (IFF, Oct–Nov 2026). FRT vendors (Innefu/Pelorus, Staqu) named in investigative press. [IFF Vajra](https://internetfreedom.in/delhi-polices-to-get-new-vajra-vans-have-six-4k-cameras-1-tb-of-storage-a-gpu-and-a-drone-deck-under-no-written-procedure/); [Medianama, Nov 2026](https://www.medianama.com/2026/11/223-delhi-police-facial-recognition-vans-beyond-protest-sites/)
- **Hikvision/Dahua**: effective sales ban on non-ER models from 1 Apr 2026; applies to new sales, not installed base. [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/industry/news/india-ban-chinese-cctv-government-security-concerns-hikvision-dahua-market-126033000316_1.html)

Inference: regulatory risk runs opposite to what a Western founder expects — the state is exempt and buying more, while the reputational risk lands on the vendor named in the tender. FRT is the highest-risk, lowest-spec'd line item; ANPR/traffic and crowd-safety are low-controversy.

## 6. Startup entry realism

**Got in**
- Staqu: direct to UP Police / UP Prisons (pilot-led, 2019–), then 11 states; small ticket sizes (Rs 19.6 cr revenue FY25 for 11 state forces + prisons implies ~Rs 1–2 cr per customer/year); now diversifying to IPL stadiums, Ram Mandir, G20 — event security is a repeat wedge. [Inc42](https://inc42.com/company/staqu/); [Business Today 2026](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/story/how-staqus-jarvis-is-securing-rcbs-home-ground-for-crowd-control-523087-2026-03-30)
- Vehant: hardware-first (baggage/UVSS scanners for PMO, airports) gave it the eligibility and balance sheet to add video analytics; rides with Intel and inside LTTS-style event ICCCs (Kumbh). [Intel case study](https://www.intel.com/content/www/us/en/content-details/852197/vehant-technologies-case-study-enhancing-crowd-detection-at-magh-mela-prayagraj-published-by-partner.html)
- Videonetics: sells VMS + analytics as the software layer that SIs (BEL, L&T, Honeywell, NEC etc.) specify; 16+ years to reach Rs 79 cr. [videonetics.ai](https://www.videonetics.ai/)
- Awiros: state-level selection (Telangana) via "AI app store" positioning; still sub-$10 mn raised after 10 years. [IndiaAI](https://indiaai.gov.in/article/how-telangana-is-averting-stampedes-through-efficient-crowd-monitoring)
- Innefu: entered Delhi Police via reseller Pelorus (2018), now reputationally exposed. [Pulitzer](https://pulitzercenter.org/stories/looking-back-camera-how-controversial-ai-firms-shaped-delhis-predictive-policing)

**Exited / pivoted**
- Uncanny Vision: sold to Eagle Eye Networks (2021) — enterprise cloud VMS, not Indian government. [EEN](https://www.een.com/blog/eagle-eye-networks-acquires-indian-ai-specialist/) **[STALE]**
- Netra, Facetagr, Zebi, Gaist: no 2025-26 government traction found (absence of evidence; search budget exhausted before deep-diving).

**Verdict (inference)**
1. A startup cannot be prime on anything > Rs 10 cr (experience walls, 5–9 year O&M, bank guarantees). Entry is as (a) analytics licensor to an MSI, (b) direct GeM seller to one police commissionerate for Rs 1–5 cr pilots, or (c) event-security supplier (Kumbh, IPL, festivals) where procurement is fast and outcome-visible.
2. Revenue ceiling for pure-analytics vendors after 8–16 years is Rs 20–80 cr; payment cycles stretch past 12 months once SPVs close. Cash-flow, not tech, kills entrants.
3. The STQC/ER shift created a once-in-a-decade opening on the **hardware** side (CP Plus doubled share in 12 months); on the **software** side the analogous lever is "VMS/analytics locally developed" in MII clauses, which favours Indian ISVs over Milestone/Genetec/BriefCam in government bids.
4. Best risk-adjusted wedges: traffic ANPR accuracy/false-challan reduction (26,000 wrong challans in Hyderabad = measurable pain, fine revenue pays for it), camera-health/uptime analytics (10–40% dead cameras, SC scrutiny), crowd-safety for events. Worst: FRT for policing (unspecified accuracy, court cases, press exposure).
5. If the founder is not willing to carry 12–18 month receivables and partner with a BEL/L&T/Honeywell-class SI, this segment is a poor fit; enterprise/retail/industrial video AI has shorter cycles.
