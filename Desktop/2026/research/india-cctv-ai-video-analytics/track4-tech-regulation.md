## 0. Executive read (for a founder)

1. **[F] The camera supply base has flipped in 18 months.** MeitY's Essential Requirements (ER-01, under the Electronics & IT Goods (Requirement of Compulsory Registration) Order) were enforced from 9 April 2025; the sale of non-ER-certified internet-connected cameras was prohibited from 1 April 2026, and the pre-2025 stock relaxation was withdrawn by Office Memorandum dated 16 Jan 2026. Hikvision- and Dahua-branded IP cameras have no STQC certificates; Prama (the Hikvision JV brand) has 58 certified models. Counterpoint puts domestic brands at >80% of sales by Feb 2026 (from ~33% a year earlier). CP Plus went from 20.8% revenue share (FY25) to an estimated 45–50% (Q4 FY26).
2. **[F] CP Plus is now a ₹6,500–6,800 crore (FY27 guidance) company with 14–15% EBITDA margins, debt-free, and ~79% of its portfolio is IP cameras** — it bundles "AI" (human/vehicle detection, intrusion, smart motion) free in the camera, and sells cloud recording at ₹150–500/camera/month. Any new entrant competes with "free, good-enough" edge analytics in the camera.
3. **[F] DPDP Rules were notified 13/14 Nov 2025; substantive obligations (notice, security safeguards, breach reporting, retention/erasure, children's data) bite on 13 May 2027.** CCTV footage of identifiable people is personal data; face recognition is biometric personal data. Employer monitoring falls under the Section 7 "legitimate uses" exemption, but notice is still required. No FRT-specific statute exists.
4. **[E] Edge costs ₹215–350 per camera per month all-in (hardware amortised + power + connectivity); continuous cloud ingest costs ₹1,200–2,900; an edge-filtered, cloud-VLM hybrid costs ₹400–700.** Breakeven for a dedicated on-prem box is ~12–16 cameras per site. Below ~8 cameras per site (kirana, clinic), cloud-on-events or in-camera AI wins.
5. **[F/I] Subsidised GPU compute exists (IndiaAI Mission: 38,000 GPUs, ~₹65/GPU-hour, up to 40% of bill covered for eligible startups), and Indian GPU clouds (E2E L4 at ₹50/hr; Yotta H100 at ₹356/hr on-demand)** are 30–50% cheaper than AWS/GCP Mumbai list for the same silicon — this makes a cloud VLM-search layer (Spot AI / Coram / Lumana style) economically viable in India for event clips, not for continuous footage.

## 1. Camera supply shift (STQC / ER-01)

### 1.1 What the rule requires
- **[F]** MeitY's Essential Requirements (ER) for CCTV under the CRO were revised on 9 April 2024 with a one-year transition; enforcement from 9 April 2025. Analog cameras exempted by MeitY notification of 12 March 2025. From 1 April 2026 no sale of non-conforming (internet-connected) cameras; the OM of 16 Jan 2026 withdrew the relaxation for pre-2025 stock. Sources: https://globalomega.com/blog/meity-clarifies-security-testing-alignment-for-cctv-cameras-under-ppp-mii-cro/ ; https://waeient.com/blog/stqc-certification-cctv-india-2026-complete-guide ; https://indo.ai/content/bis-er-01-cctv-buyer-guide.html (2026).
- **[F]** Six headline requirements: no default passwords; encrypted video streams; secure boot with signed firmware; debug ports disabled; **chipset/component origin disclosure**; vulnerability-disclosure policy. Testing covers hardware (tamper), software/firmware (SBOM, source-code-level security review), process (trusted supply chain). Source: https://www.diligencecertification.com/stqc-certification-for-cctv-cameras/ ; https://www.matrixcomsec.com/stqc-certification-er-compliance-for-cctv-cameras/
- **[F]** Cost and time: ER-01 testing ≈ ₹5.5 lakh average per product series (₹3–8 lakh range); BIS fee ₹30,000 per application + ₹20,000 per additional test report; lab testing 4–8 weeks + 1–2 weeks committee; realistic 2–3 months per model. Sources: https://instacertify.com/blog/stqc-certification-cost/ ; https://fgtechstore.com/blog/stqc-certification-cost-timeline-cctv/ (2026).

### 1.4 Implications for a new entrant
- **[I]** Design for the **post-2026 fleet: CP Plus / Prama / Matrix / Sparsh / Qubo IP cameras**, which are overwhelmingly ONVIF + RTSP (Dahua-lineage firmware). Support legacy Hikvision/Dahua via RTSP/ISAPI for brownfield, but expect those to be swapped out in government and regulated accounts.
- **[I]** CP Plus's 70%+ gross-margin software layer is thin today (86 R&D engineers, "AI" = in-camera detectors). The margin pool it is not capturing — cross-site search, workflow/compliance analytics, VLM search — is the opening. The risk is CP Plus bundling a cloud analytics tier through its 30,000 partners.
- **[I]** Any hardware you ship (edge box) is NOT a "CCTV camera" under ER-01, but check CRO coverage for the box category (likely IT equipment under IS 13252 safety registration, not ER cybersecurity). If you ship a camera, budget ₹5–8 lakh and 3 months per model for ER certification.

## 4. Data protection and legal

### 4.1 DPDP Act 2023 and DPDP Rules 2025
- **[F]** Rules notified 13/14 Nov 2025 (G.S.R., MeitY; PIB doc dated 14 Nov 2025). Phasing: Rules 1, 2, 17–21 (Board constitution) immediate; Rule 4 (Consent Manager registration) at 12 months (Nov 2026); Rules 3, 5–16, 22–23 (notice, security safeguards, breach intimation, retention/erasure, children, SDF, cross-border, grievance) at 18 months = **13 May 2027**. ## 7. Structural shifts that create an opening (ranked)
1. **STQC wiped out the Chinese IP-camera supply (Apr 2026)** → a homogeneous, ONVIF/RTSP, Dahua-lineage domestic fleet (CP Plus ~45–50%) with thin software; government and regulated buyers are actively replacing ~1 million cameras.
2. **DPDP substantive obligations on 13 May 2027** → every CCTV operator becomes a Data Fiduciary needing notices, retention schedules, breach handling, processor contracts; vendors that ship compliance tooling (signage QR, auto-erasure, audit logs, face-blur) get pulled in.
3. **VLM-based natural-language video search has arrived globally (Conntour $7M, Spot AI, Coram, Lumana) and is unoccupied in India** — incumbents are detector-rule vendors; Indian GPU clouds + IndiaAI subsidy make clip-level VLM inference ≈ ₹25–40/camera/month.
4. **Labour Codes (21 Nov 2025) and state school/hostel/PG mandates** convert CCTV from "security" to "compliance evidence" (OSH, POSH, child safety), creating recurring-revenue analytics use cases beyond theft.
5. **Camera prices rose 15–20% on BoM, but edge compute fell** (Orin Nano Super ₹45–50k, Hailo-8 ₹16–21k) → a ₹50k box serving 10–16 cameras at ~₹200–350/camera/month undercuts cloud recording plans (₹150–500/camera/month) while adding analytics.

## 8. Compliance must-haves checklist (design-time)
- [ ] Cameras you sell/recommend: on the STQC ER-01 certified list (BIS CRO); avoid non-certified imports entirely post 1 Apr 2026.
- [ ] Data Processor posture: contracts with customers (fiduciaries) covering purpose limitation, retention, sub-processors, breach notification SLA; no cross-customer training without consent.
- [ ] Notice: signage generator with QR → itemised purposes, retention, grievance officer; in-app notice templates; employee notice under Section 7.
- [ ] Retention and erasure: per-site configurable (RBI 90/180-day presets), automatic deletion, deletion logs.
- [ ] Security safeguards (Rule 6): encryption at rest/in transit, RBAC, access audit logs, India data residency option, breach detection and Board/principal notification workflow.
- [ ] Children: block FRT/attendance by default for under-18 sites; verifiable parental consent flow if enabled.
- [ ] FRT: opt-in, enrolled-subject only, on-prem templates, face-blur on export, DPIA template for SDF-class customers.
- [ ] No cameras/analytics in washrooms, changing rooms, PG rooms (state HC guidelines, labour rules).
- [ ] Police-sharing (Nenu Saitham-style) and law-enforcement requests: documented process (Section 17 basis), export watermarking.
